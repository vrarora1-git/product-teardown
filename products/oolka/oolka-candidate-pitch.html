What I saw using the product

Three moments that break trust

A payment demand on a closed loan. A WhatsApp message asking for ₹10,712 on a loan already fully paid, with a Pay Now button and a payment guarantee attached. One false demand like this undoes months of earned trust.

A payment screen with no way to pay. A countdown timer running with zero payment methods loaded and no retry or help path. Urgency with no action is the worst version of a critical path.

Good advice with no next step. A utilization alert that was accurate and personal, but sent on WhatsApp with no explanation of why it matters and no link to act. Right idea, wrong surface, no follow through.

The hidden truth

The gap and the asset, in one line

Today Oolka is a notification product wearing an agent's badge. The fix is already on the shelf: secured cards from IDFC First, Yes Bank and AU sit in the marketplace as inventory. A secured card is the one instrument where the score verifiably moves month after month, which is exactly what the paid tier promises and what notifications alone cannot deliver. Nudges get dismissed. Products get used.

The pattern: every issue above is the same issue. Advice fires without validation, urgency fires without an action path, and the agent promises more than the rails beneath it can do. The answer is to build the rails. That is page 3.

My first 90 days

Trust first, then the repayment spine, then the products that move scores. Each phase ships and each one measurably moves a number.

Days 0 to 30 · Trust

Make every touch verifiable

  • Consent vault: every permission captured with a 6 month validity, visible in one screen, with a one tap option to disable. Users should never wonder what they agreed to.
  • Autopay done right: explicit mandate setup, pre debit notification, easy pause. This directly answers the mandate complaints in reviews. I scaled 1M to 5M mandates at Paytm; trust is what made that curve possible.
  • Alerts that validate before they fire: account status check as step zero, so a closed loan never gets a payment demand. Every alert carries the why and a one tap action.
  • Downloadable branded report: improvement notes included, shareable with lenders. Tangible proof of progress that users can hold.
  • Plus the score clarity fix: name the bureau on screen and explain why it differs from CIBIL.
Days 31 to 60 · The repayment spine

One screen for everything owed

  • Repayment dashboard: every loan, card, due date and minimum due in one view, built on bureau plus Account Aggregator signals, paid through the BBPS rails Oolka already has.
  • Payment prioritisation: when cash is tight, tell the user what to pay first and why. This is the screen users open weekly, and it ends repaying blind across five apps.
  • Dispute resolution with a closed loop: in app status tracking until the bureau resolves, human escalation for hard cases. The loop after filing is where users decide you are real.
Days 61 to 90 · Products that move scores

Credit builder as the hero, score as the key

  • Secured card as the credit builder: route new to credit and subprime users into the cards already on the platform, with guided milestones and monthly bureau reporting. I took this exact product from term sheet to 20K+ cards and an 80% active rate.
  • What your score unlocks: for stronger scores, turn the number into offers, upgrades and rates. Distribution stops being a banner and becomes the natural next step.

One base, four different users

A thin file user and a 750 user need different products, different nudges and different reasons to pay. Treating them as one audience wastes the base.

SegmentWhat they need mostWhat converts them

Subprime (300 to 500)Repair: overdue detection, dispute help, multi bureau compare to catch reporting gapsStep by step repair plan, in app repayment of overdues, score boost rewards

Free tier stays the hook: score, repayment, rewards, credit builder, offers. The paid tier earns its price with the depth features: multi bureau, simulator, coach, alerts, EMI tracking, disputes.
